**2026.33.1**

**VRCN Theme**
* Updated the Main VRCN theme from "Slates" to "VRCN" which is a dark black/grey-ish and blue-ish accent styled theme optimized for late night sessions.

**Multi-Account**
* When using a different account and starting VRChat through VRCN, VRChat will now use your currently selected profile to log in.
* Keep in mind that on the first launch, you may need to log back into your VRChat account.
* Added "GAME PROFILE X" Indicator to Settings > Accounts

**Fixes**
* Fixed instance sessions in Timeline > Personal staying "Ongoing" forever when VRChat was already closed.
* Fixed a wrong "Ongoing" instance entry being created for the active account after switching accounts in VRCN and restarting, even though that account was never in the instance.
